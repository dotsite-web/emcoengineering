Our Journey

Built on Experience,
Proven by Time

Emco Engineering Pvt Ltd was founded with a single mission: provide Sri Lankan homes and businesses with security and audio systems that genuinely last. Two decades later, that promise still holds.

2004
Emco Engineering Founded
Nirosh Caldera establishes Emco Engineering Pvt Ltd in Pannipitiya, beginning with CCTV and security alarm installations for local residential and commercial clients.
2006 – 2010
Expanding into Audio
Growing client demand leads Emco to add distributed audio and PA systems to its portfolio. Partnerships with key suppliers enable competitive equipment pricing.
2011 – 2016
Commercial & Retail Growth
Emco becomes the preferred systems integrator for Sarasavi Bookshops and Arpico branches across Sri Lanka, delivering CCTV and audio installations island-wide.
2017 – Present
Full-Service Integration
Emco now offers a complete service portfolio — CCTV, security alarms, distributed audio, PA systems, audio visual, PABX, and ongoing repairs & maintenance — serving clients island-wide.
